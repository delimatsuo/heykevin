## What this build contains

- Country-aware sign-up phone entry and validation.
- Safer market admission: a legacy production backend that lacks the market
  catalog remains explicitly **US-only**; transport failures, 5xx responses and
  malformed catalog responses fail closed.
- US-only forwarding actions with stronger country and destination checks.
  Canada shows carrier qualification and restoration guidance, never generic
  dial codes.
- Native Jobber connection management: status, connect, reconnect, refresh and
  disconnect states, with account-bound handling and safe unavailable/error
  states.
- Localized StoreKit subscription prices in the paywall.
- Guarded Canadian owner-SMS consent UI: it is shown only when the server
  provides its dedicated capability; an older backend leaves the setting
  unavailable rather than presenting a nonfunctional opt-in.

## Limits retained for this TestFlight build

Brazil and the United Kingdom are not enabled markets. Canadian forwarding
activation and Canadian SMS enrollment are not enabled. Jobber operations
require the matching deployed and configured backend; this iOS package alone
does not activate them. The Cloud Run worker optimization is not part of the
binary and was not deployed. No live phone call, carrier forwarding action,
text, number purchase, App Store public release, or external beta delivery was
performed.

All task-owned archives, IPA files, extracts and raw Apple logs were held only
under the configured temporary directory and removed after Apple readback.
