# Voice bakeoff development corpus

This checked-in manifest is development-only and unsealed. It contains no audio,
scripts, transcripts, phone identities, provider payloads, credentials, or caller
data. Its scenario identifiers define coverage requirements only.

Synthetic phone-codec fixtures are the default. Purpose-recorded consenting-adult
audio is prohibited until a separate approval records consent digest, rights,
encryption, TTL, adjudication, withdrawal handling, and deletion-residue proof.
Real calls, staging calls, production calls, and customer material are prohibited.

No finite language or code-switching contract has been selected. That is an owner
decision before sealing; an empty list here is intentional and cannot support a
release claim. The external sealed holdout is also intentionally absent from the
repository until its access-controlled custody is approved.
