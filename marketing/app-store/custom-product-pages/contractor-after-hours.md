## Promotional text

Missed calls can be new jobs. Kevin answers routed calls, qualifies urgency, captures the customer's details, and lets you take over live.

## Creative promise

Lead with the job-to-be-done: the owner cannot always stop working to answer. Show the live intake first, prioritized recents second, and the full caller context third.

Do not promise automatic booking, guaranteed revenue, or 24/7 coverage unless the live product configuration supports those claims for the advertised plan.
