# Personal call-screening Custom Product Page

- Reference name: `personal-call-screening`
- Audience hypothesis: people seeking help with unknown calls for personal use
- Apple Ads campaign: proposed Personal Exact experiment; keep paused until release and activation acceptance

## Promotional text

Let Kevin ask who is calling and why. Screen unknown callers, read live transcripts, and review summaries when it suits you. Carrier forwarding setup required.

## Creative promise

Lead with control and interruption reduction. The first screenshot demonstrates live screening, the second shows useful summaries plus spam handling, and the third shows the context available before a callback.

Suggested captions for a fresh, verified screenshot set:

1. **Know who's calling and why.** Kevin screens calls routed to your Kevin number.
2. **Catch up on your time.** Review recent calls and their summaries.
3. **Get the context before calling back.** Read what the caller said.

Use personal examples such as a delivery, school or pharmacy callback. Existing
Debug fixtures contain fictional examples; capture actual app screens, then check
legibility and feature accuracy. Do not imply guaranteed spam blocking, automatic
carrier setup or a completed call that has only been observed inbound.

Before publication, verify carrier availability, trial eligibility and the prices
shown by the release build and Apple purchase sheet. Copy approval and saved local
assets do not establish App Review approval or live ad delivery.
